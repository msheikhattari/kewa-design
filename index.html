“Clothes should fit the life, not just the body.”

Asal Dianat is a stylist and couture designer working across two worlds: collection work through the studio, and independent commissions for private clients — couture pieces, styling appointments, and made-to-order garments under the Kewa label.

Trained in pattern-making and construction, she works from fitting to finish. Studio clients get collection-level polish; private clients get a piece nobody else has.
