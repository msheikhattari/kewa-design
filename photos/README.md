# Adding photos — quick guide

1. Tap **Add file → Upload files** (top right of the file list).
2. Drag photos in, or tap "choose your files" to pick from your phone or computer.
3. Scroll down and press the green **Commit changes** button.

Done — they will be picked up and worked into the site.

**Tip:** name files so we know what each look is, e.g. `bridal-01.jpg`,
`couture-gown-02.jpg`, `styling-client-03.jpg`. Straight-from-phone photos
are fine; resizing and compression are handled for you.
